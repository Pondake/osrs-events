OSRS Events

Clan events worth showing up for.

A free place to run Old School RuneScape events for your clan — set one up, share a link, and watch the standings move while everyone plays.

  • Snakes & Ladders A board of any size with an OSRS task on every tile. Roll a daily d6; ladders jump you ahead, snakes drop you back.
  • Skill & drop races Pick a skill or a boss and race on what everyone gains. The leaderboard reads the hiscores and updates itself while you watch.
  • Bingo A card of drops and goals, claimed with a screenshot and signed off by the host. Line or full house.
